Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

This website ("My World") is a personal vlog. Your privacy matters. This page explains what information is collected and how it is used.

What information is collected

This website does not collect personal information automatically. The only personal data collected is what you voluntarily submit through the contact form (your name, email address, and message).

How your information is used

Information submitted through the contact form is used only to respond to your message. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for marketing purposes.

Cookies

This website uses only one item of local storage: your colour theme preference (light or dark mode). This is stored in your browser's localStorage and never sent to any server. No tracking cookies or advertising cookies are used.

Third-party services

This website uses Google Fonts, which loads font files from Google's servers. Google may collect usage data according to their own privacy policy. If you use the contact form, messages are processed through Formspree (or your configured form provider) according to their privacy policy.

Analytics

No analytics service (Google Analytics, etc.) is used on this website by default. If you add analytics in the future, update this policy accordingly.

Data retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as needed to respond to your message. Theme preference data stays in your own browser and can be cleared by you at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to request deletion of any personal data submitted through the contact form. To do so, please send a message via the contact page.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the contact page.
